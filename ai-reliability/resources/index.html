Sample scorecard

A fictional support assistant drafts refund replies. The reviewer compares three invented cases with a sample refund policy. Each rating describes the evidence available for that control.

ControlRatingObservation and action
Source accuracyPartialTwo drafts cite the policy. One promises an exception without a source. Require a policy reference before release.
Human approvalDemonstratedAll three drafts waited for an agent. Retain the approval step and record who approved each reply.
RecoveryUnverifiedNo outage test was supplied. Have the workflow owner rehearse routing a failed request to a person.

Rating guide. Demonstrated means observed in the sample. Partial means inconsistent. Unverified means evidence is missing. A failure means the control was tested and did not work.
