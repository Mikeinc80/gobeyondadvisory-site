AI Reliability Snapshot

A fixed US$750 introductory review of one AI workflow. Receive a scorecard, evidence notes, and three prioritized actions for the person who owns the workflow.

What the review covers

  1. Scope. Agree on one use case, its intended users and the decision it supports.
  2. Test. Review up to ten representative, redacted examples against agreed expectations. Include one ambiguous input and one failure case.
  3. Review together. Receive a written scorecard and a 30 minute readout covering observed failures, evidence gaps, ownership and next steps.

The Snapshot excludes implementation, penetration testing, legal opinions, certification and continuous monitoring.
