Our evidence standard

A stage is complete when we hold the document that proves it.

Regulatory status is the single easiest thing for an early-stage infrastructure company to overstate, and the single most damaging thing to get wrong. So we apply one rule to this page.

A stage moves from pending to complete only when EKORails LTD holds written evidence of it — a certificate, an acknowledgement, a decision letter or an executed agreement — and the page records the date it changed and what changed it.

If the Central Bank of Nigeria confirms a status, this page will state the exact status granted, in the regulator’s own terms, and nothing beyond it. Until then, this page says applied.

What a sandbox is

A supervised test with limits — not permission to operate.

What sandbox participation would mean

  • Testing a defined proposition with a defined participant group.
  • Operating within value, volume and duration caps set by the regulator.
  • Reporting to the regulator at an agreed frequency and in an agreed format.
  • Supervision of the test, including the power to stop it.
  • A defined exit at the end of the test period.

What sandbox participation would not mean

  • It is not a commercial operating licence.
  • It is not authorisation to serve the general public.
  • It is not an endorsement, approval or recommendation of EKORails by the regulator.
  • It is not a guarantee that a licence will follow.
  • It does not transfer regulated responsibilities from licensed partners to EKORails.

How we will describe an outcome

If admission is confirmed, this website will be updated to state the exact status granted by the Central Bank of Nigeria, the date it was granted, the scope it covers and the conditions attached, and will continue to state clearly that the status is not a commercial operating licence unless the regulator says otherwise. If the application is unsuccessful or withdrawn, this page will say so.
