Platform

A coordination layer for regulated cross-border transactions.

EKORails is being designed to sit between the institutions that already hold licences, funds and market access — orchestrating the sequence, enforcing the controls and holding the record, without taking on the functions that licensed institutions are there to perform.

▲ The platform is in development. No component described on this page is operating in production or carrying customer value. Component status labels are applied consistently throughout.

Platform overview

A cross-border business payment is not one action. It is a sequence of dependent actions performed by different institutions under different rules, where any step can fail and the whole must still be explainable afterwards.

Most of the difficulty in a corridor is not the movement of value itself. It is holding that sequence together: knowing which party is responsible for the current step, what data they need, what they returned, what the customer should be told, and what the supervisor will later expect to see.

EKORails is being built as that coordination layer. It orchestrates the steps, enforces the agreed controls before an instruction is routed, and writes an append-only record of every state change. Funds, conversion, settlement finality and payout remain with licensed institutions.

Transaction lifecycle

Eleven states, each of which can be evidenced.

A transaction is only ever in one state. Every transition is written to the audit record with the actor, the timestamp and the reason. Failure states are first-class: a transaction that cannot proceed must resolve to a defined outcome, not disappear.

API and messaging layer

The interface is designed around a small number of resources — participants, beneficiaries, instructions, quotes, transactions, cases and reports — with idempotent submission so that a retried instruction cannot create a duplicate payment.

Message content is aligned to ISO 20022 semantics so that the data a partner bank needs arrives in the form its systems already expect, rather than in a proprietary shape that has to be mapped by hand. [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS — message standard as filed]

Status is delivered by signed webhook with a polling fallback, because a partner that misses a callback must still be able to establish the truth of a transaction independently.

Ledger and record-keeping approach

EKORails maintains an append-only record of transaction state. Entries are written once, never edited; a correction is a new entry that references the entry it corrects, so the history of what was known and when remains intact.

This record is an operational and evidential ledger, not a store of customer value. Balances of customer funds are held and reported by the licensed institutions that hold them.

Retention periods follow the longest applicable requirement across the markets in the corridor. [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS — retention schedule]

The settlement asset, mechanism and timing proposed for the pilot corridor are those set out in EKORails LTD’s regulatory filing. [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS — settlement asset and mechanism as filed] [SUBJECT TO REGULATORY APPROVAL]

Three commitments hold regardless of the mechanism finally approved. Settlement finality is provided by a licensed institution, not asserted by EKORails. Customer funds sit with licensed institutions throughout. And the settlement leg is recorded with enough detail that both sides and their supervisors can reconstruct it independently.

EKORails does not issue, offer, promote or sell any token, coin or investment instrument, and no settlement mechanism described here is offered to the public as an asset.

Compliance controls

Controls are enforced before routing, not reported after the fact. An instruction that breaches a limit, fails screening or lacks required party data does not proceed to a partner; it becomes a case with an owner and a clock.

The full framework — governance, AML and CFT, KYC and KYB, sanctions, monitoring, fraud, data protection, cybersecurity, resilience, third-party risk, retention and complaints — is set out on the Compliance and Risk page.

Resilience and business continuity

The design assumption is that partners and networks will be unavailable at some point, and that a payment stuck between two institutions is worse than a payment that was never accepted.

  • Defined recovery time and recovery point objectives per component. [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS — RTO/RPO targets]
  • Degraded-mode behaviour: stop accepting new instructions for an affected route rather than accept and hold indefinitely.
  • Reconciliation-first recovery: establish the true state with each partner before resuming.
  • Tested restore procedures and a documented incident and communications plan.
  • An exit and wind-down plan so that participants are not stranded if the pilot ends.

Insolvency and wind-down

Because EKORails does not hold customer funds, an insolvency of EKORails LTD does not put customer money at risk in the design: funds sit with licensed institutions throughout, and the claim a customer holds is against that institution under its own regime, not against EKORails.

What an EKORails failure would put at risk is coordination and record-keeping. The wind-down plan therefore covers three obligations: stop accepting new instructions immediately; drive every in-flight transaction to a terminal state — settled, returned or rejected — with each partner; and hand participants and their supervisors a complete, readable export of the audit record for the full retention period.

The reverse direction matters equally. If a settlement, conversion or payout partner fails, the platform must be able to establish the true state of every transaction on that route by reconciliation rather than assumption, and to stop routing to it without stranding work already accepted. Concentration on a single partner for any critical function is treated as a risk in its own right.

Data handling

Personal and transaction data is collected on a defined lawful basis, minimised to what the transaction and the applicable rules require, encrypted in transit and at rest, segregated between environments, and retained to a published schedule.
