2. What we collect

  • Information you give us in a form. Name, position, organisation, country, work email, organisation type, proposed partnership, licence or regulatory status, expected corridor, expected volume range, your message and your consent record.
  • Correspondence. Emails and messages you send us and our replies.
  • Measurement data, where you allow it. Pages viewed, referring source, approximate country, device type and interactions such as which calls to action are used.
  • Technical data required to serve the site. IP address, request time and user agent, processed by our hosting and security providers to deliver pages and prevent abuse.

We do not collect payment details, government identity documents or financial account information through these websites. We have no retail customers and do not process consumer payment data.

7. Your rights

Subject to the law that applies to you, you may request access to your personal data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, or object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw consent at any time without affecting processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise a right, email compliance@ekorails.com. We respond within the period required by applicable law. Target response time: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]

You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority, including the Nigeria Data Protection Commission where applicable.

8. Cookies and measurement

These websites use no advertising cookies and no cross-site tracking. We use a privacy-preserving measurement tool to understand which pages are read, and we ask for your choice before enabling it. Your choice is stored in your browser and can be changed at any time by clearing site data. Measurement provider and cookie table: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]

9. Security

We apply access control, encryption in transit and at rest, logging and vendor assessment to the personal data we hold. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your rights we will notify you and the relevant authority as required by law.

10. Changes

We update this policy when our processing changes. The date at the top shows the current version. Material changes affecting how we use data you have already given us will be notified to you directly where we hold contact details for you.
