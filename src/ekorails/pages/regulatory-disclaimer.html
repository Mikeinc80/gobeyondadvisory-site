Summary

EKORails LTD is developing financial infrastructure for institutional and enterprise use. Information on this website is provided for general and partnership purposes and does not constitute financial, investment, legal or payment-services advice. Products, pilot activities and services described may be subject to regulatory review, licensing, partner approval and geographic restrictions.

1. Licensing and authorisation status

EKORails LTD is not licensed, authorised, approved, endorsed, registered or supervised by the Central Bank of Nigeria or by any other financial services regulator, in any jurisdiction.

EKORails LTD does not hold a banking licence, a payment service provider licence, an international money transfer operator licence, an e-money licence, a money transmission licence, an investment business authorisation or any equivalent authorisation.

EKORails LTD does not accept deposits, does not hold client money, does not provide payment services to the public and does not operate a settlement system.

2. Regulatory sandbox application

EKORails LTD has applied to participate in the Central Bank of Nigeria Regulatory Sandbox. Participation and all proposed pilot activities remain subject to regulatory review and approval.

An application is not an admission, an approval, a licence or an endorsement. If EKORails LTD is admitted to the sandbox, that admission would be a supervised testing arrangement with a defined scope, defined limits and defined reporting obligations. It would not be a commercial operating licence and would not authorise EKORails LTD to offer services to the public outside the approved test.

If a status is granted, this website will be updated to state the exact status granted, the date it was granted, the scope it covers and the conditions attached. If the application is refused or withdrawn, this website will say so.

Nothing on this website should be interpreted as suggesting that the Central Bank of Nigeria has reviewed, approved or endorsed EKORails LTD, its platform, its documentation or any statement made by it.

References to what the platform is “designed to” do, is “intended to” do, or would do “subject to regulatory approval” describe design intent, not current function.
