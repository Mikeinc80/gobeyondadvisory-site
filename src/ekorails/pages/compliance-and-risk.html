Compliance and risk

The control framework, written for the people who will test it.

This page describes the framework EKORails LTD is building and would operate under an approved pilot. It is written to be read by supervisors, bank second-line functions and enterprise risk teams, and to be checked against what we actually do.

▲ EKORails LTD holds no compliance, security or quality certification and claims none. No statement on this page should be read as certification, accreditation or regulatory approval. Where an independent audit or certification is completed, it will be named, dated and scoped.

Governance

Accountability for compliance sits with a named compliance officer who reports to the board of EKORails LTD and has the authority to stop a transaction, a participant or a release. Name, qualifications and appointment date: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]

The board reviews the risk register, incidents, open regulatory matters and control exceptions on a defined cycle. Committee structure and cadence: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]

Policies are written, versioned, approved and reviewed at least annually. Every policy has a named owner. Where a policy has not yet been approved, it is treated as draft and the control it describes is not claimed as operating.

Regulatory engagement

EKORails LTD has applied to participate in the Central Bank of Nigeria Regulatory Sandbox. Participation and all proposed pilot activities remain subject to regulatory review and approval. EKORails LTD is not licensed, authorised, approved, endorsed or supervised by any financial services regulator.

Engagement is conducted through the named compliance officer, with a single record of correspondence, commitments given and information provided, so that what we told a regulator and what we tell the market cannot drift apart.

Public statements about regulatory status are subject to pre-publication review. See the regulatory pathway.

AML and CFT programme

A written anti-money-laundering and counter-financing-of-terrorism programme covering: a documented business-wide risk assessment; policies and procedures proportionate to that assessment; a named responsible officer; training for all staff with role-specific training for those in control functions; and independent testing of the programme.

The programme is designed against Nigerian requirements and the FATF Recommendations, and against the requirements applying to partners in the receiving market. Where a licensed partner owns the regulated decision, EKORails supports it with complete data and evidence rather than substituting its own judgement.

KYC and KYB

Every participating institution and business is verified before it can transact: legal identity, registration, regulatory status where applicable, beneficial ownership, directors and authorised signatories, and the nature and expected pattern of its corridor activity.

Individuals acting for a participant are identity-verified and their authority evidenced. Verification is refreshed on a risk-based cycle, and material changes — ownership, control, banking details — trigger re-verification rather than a note on file.

Evidence is retained in a form that a partner or supervisor can inspect.

Sanctions

Parties and payment data are screened at onboarding and at transaction time against applicable sanctions and watchlists for both markets in the corridor, including lists that partners are separately obliged to apply.

Matches are not auto-cleared. A potential match holds the instruction, creates a case with an owner and a service level, and is dispositioned by a person whose decision is recorded with its rationale. List coverage and final disposition responsibility sit with the licensed partner of record.

Screening provider and list coverage: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]

Transaction monitoring

Corridor activity is monitored against rules and thresholds calibrated to the expected pattern for each participant: value, frequency, counterparties, timing, structuring indicators and deviation from a participant’s own baseline.

Alerts route to a case-managed queue with ownership and timelines. Rules are tuned on evidence, and every change to a rule is versioned so that a supervisor can see what was being monitored on any given date.

EKORails does not claim that monitoring detects all illicit activity. No monitoring programme can, and any statement to the contrary would be false.

Fraud prevention

Controls target the fraud types that actually affect corridor payments: invoice redirection and beneficiary-detail changes, business email compromise, account takeover of a participant’s portal user, and mule beneficiary accounts.

Measures include change-of-detail verification through a separate channel, beneficiary whitelisting, dual approval above defined thresholds, device and session controls, and anomaly detection on instruction patterns.

Suspicious activity escalation

Defined escalation paths and timelines from the analyst to the named compliance officer, and from the compliance officer to the relevant authority through the licensed partner of record where the reporting obligation sits with that partner.

Tipping-off restrictions are respected: escalation procedures are designed so that a participant is not told anything that would prejudice an investigation.

Data protection

Processing is carried out on a defined lawful basis, minimised to what the transaction and applicable rules require, and retained to a published schedule. Data subject rights — access, rectification, erasure where applicable, objection and portability — are handled through a documented process with a response deadline.

Cross-border transfers within a corridor are assessed against the regimes of both markets, including the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 where applicable, and the UK and EU regimes where relevant to the entity or its processors.

Cybersecurity

The security programme is built on least-privilege access with multi-factor authentication, encryption in transit and at rest, environment segregation, centralised logging and alerting, dependency and vulnerability management, secrets management, and secure development practices including peer review and automated testing.

Independent penetration testing is performed before any pilot and on a defined cycle thereafter; findings are tracked to closure. Testing provider, scope and last test date: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]

Specific configuration details are not published, because publishing them would weaken the controls they describe. They are made available to partners and reviewers under NDA.

Operational resilience

Resilience is planned around the assumption that partners and networks will be unavailable at some point. Recovery objectives are defined per component; degraded-mode behaviour stops acceptance on an affected route rather than accepting instructions that cannot complete; recovery begins with reconciliation against each partner.

Continuity plans are tested, not merely written, and the test results are recorded. RTO and RPO targets and last test date: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]

Third-party and partner risk

Every partner and vendor is assessed before engagement — licensing and regulatory standing, financial condition, security posture, data protection, subcontracting and concentration risk — and reassessed on a risk-based cycle.

Contracts set out responsibilities, service levels, audit and information rights, incident notification timelines, data protection terms and exit provisions. Concentration on any single partner for a critical function is treated as a risk in its own right.

Record retention

Transaction records, verification evidence, screening and monitoring outcomes, case records and communications are retained for the longest applicable period across the markets in the corridor, and are recoverable in a readable form for the whole of that period.

Retention schedule: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]

Complaints and dispute handling

Where a participant’s customer has a complaint, the regulated complaints process belongs to the licensed institution that holds that customer relationship. EKORails supports it with transaction evidence, status and timelines, to an agreed service level.

Complaints made directly to EKORails by participants are logged, acknowledged, owned and resolved against published timelines, with root causes fed back into controls. Complaints contact and target response time: [CONFIRM WITH EKORAILS]
