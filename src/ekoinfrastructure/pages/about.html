About Eko Infrastructure.

A research and policy platform for African settlement infrastructure, operated by a company that is building in the same market. We think that is workable, provided the interest is disclosed and the method is strict.

What this platform is for

Decisions about payment infrastructure in African markets are made by people who need accurate descriptions of how the existing systems work — not by people who need to be persuaded that everything is broken. Much of the available commentary does the second.

Eko Infrastructure publishes the first: careful explanations of clearing, settlement, corridors, compliance and policy, with sources and dates, written for institutions, policymakers and the teams inside them.

Editorial standards

Six standards govern what appears here.

  • Named authorship. Every article carries a named author. Anonymous institutional voice is not used to lend authority.
  • Dates that mean something. Publication date and last-updated date are both shown, and the second changes only when the content changes.
  • Sources on every claim. Statistics are cited to a primary source with a period and a retrieval date, and listed in the source register.
  • Fair description of others. Other systems and organisations are described factually, including what they do well. We do not publish attacks on named industry participants.
  • Separation from marketing. Research pages do not carry product calls to action beyond a clearly marked link, and no article is written to support a commercial announcement.
  • Visible corrections. Material corrections are noted at the foot of the piece rather than made silently.

Review workflow

Every piece moves through four states before publication, and the state is recorded against the article in the content management system so that an editor can see at a glance what is waiting on what.

Editorial workflow. Market data and regulatory statements can be updated without rebuilding the website.
StateWhat happensWho
DraftWritten, with sources captured as the piece is drafted rather than added afterwards.Author
Source checkEvery figure verified against its primary source; register entries created or updated; retrieval dates set.Editor
Regulatory reviewAny statement touching regulatory status, licensing, partners or platform capability is reviewed against the evidence standard.Named compliance officer
PublishedLive, with publication and last-updated dates set and a scheduled review date recorded.Editor

Pieces containing market data or regulatory statements carry a scheduled review date. When that date passes, the piece is flagged for re-verification, because a correct figure from two years ago is a wrong figure today.
