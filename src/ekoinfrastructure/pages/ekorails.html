What EKORails is building

EKORails LTD is developing a compliance-first settlement and interoperability platform designed to help regulated institutions and businesses move value across selected African and global trade corridors.

In the terms used elsewhere on this site, it is being designed as an orchestration and record layer: it coordinates the sequence of a cross-border transaction, enforces agreed controls before an instruction is routed, and holds an append-only record of what happened. It is not designed to hold customer funds, act as principal in currency conversion, provide settlement finality or operate a payout licence — those functions remain with licensed institutions.

The platform is in development. No capability is operating in production and no corridor is operating.

Regulatory status

EKORails LTD has applied to participate in the Central Bank of Nigeria Regulatory Sandbox. Participation and all proposed pilot activities remain subject to regulatory review and approval.

EKORails LTD is not licensed, authorised, approved, endorsed or supervised by the Central Bank of Nigeria or by any other financial services regulator, and holds no payment services, banking, e-money or money transmission licence in any jurisdiction.

The current stage-by-stage status is published on the EKORails regulatory pathway page and is only advanced when documentary evidence supports it.

The relationship between the two platforms

EKORails.com is the corporate and platform website: what the company is building, its regulatory pathway, its compliance framework and how to partner with it.

EkoInfrastructure.com is the research, policy and market-intelligence platform. It explains the market rather than selling into it, and it is where the company’s claims about the market can be checked against sources.

The separation is editorial, not legal: both are operated by EKORails LTD. What the separation buys a reader is that research pages are held to the editorial standards published here, and are not written to support commercial announcements.

How we handle the conflict

An operator with a commercial interest publishing research about its own market has a conflict. Three practices manage it, and they are checkable.

  • Disclosure on every page. The footer of every page on this site states who operates it and what their regulatory status is.
  • Fair treatment of alternatives. Where an existing system serves a corridor well, we say so — including where that makes the commercial case for EKORails weaker.
  • Separate claim standards. Statements about EKORails’ own status and capability are held to a documentary evidence standard, reviewed by the named compliance officer before publication.

If you think a piece here reads as advocacy rather than analysis, tell us at media@ekoinfrastructure.com. That feedback is treated as a correction request.
