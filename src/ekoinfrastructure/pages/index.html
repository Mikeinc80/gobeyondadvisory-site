Who publishes this

Eko Infrastructure is operated by EKORails LTD, a company developing settlement infrastructure. It is a commercially operated knowledge platform, not an independent academic or standards body, and it should be read with that interest in mind.

Our response to that is method: every claim is sourced, every piece is dated and revision-tracked, and where a competing system performs better than ours would, we say so. Editorial standards.
