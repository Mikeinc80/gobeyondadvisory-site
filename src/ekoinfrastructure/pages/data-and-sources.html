Reference

Data and sources.

Every statistic published across Eko Infrastructure and EKORails is listed here with its source, its date and its limitations. If a figure is not in this register, we do not publish it.

Sourcing rules

Six rules we hold ourselves to.

Rule 01

Primary source or nothing

We cite the body that produced the data, not an article about it. Where only a secondary source exists, we say so.

Rule 02

Date everything

Every figure carries the period it covers and the date we retrieved it. Payment data ages quickly.

Rule 03

Definitions travel with numbers

What was counted, and how, is published alongside the figure. Most disagreements are definitional.

Rule 04

Estimates are labelled

Where we model, we show the inputs, assumptions and arithmetic, and call the output an estimate.

Rule 05

No superlatives without a dataset

“Largest”, “fastest growing” and multiples require a named dataset, a year and a definition.

Rule 06

Placeholders, not guesses

Where a figure is required but unverified, we publish a visible placeholder rather than an approximation.

Claims we do not make

The following statements are not published on either website because they are unsupported, misleading, or comparative attacks rather than analysis: that PAPSS has near-zero adoption; that any product is a “PAPSS replacement”; that SWIFT universally takes three to five business days; that stablecoin settlement eliminates FX spread loss entirely; that costs are “cents on the dollar” without published modelling; that every revenue dollar stays in Africa; that every transaction is compliant; that architecture is complete; that any corridor is active; or that any corridor is the largest of its kind or carries a multiple of another, absent a cited dataset. Where an earlier EKORails or ECO-branded publication made such a claim, it is withdrawn.

Corrections

If you believe something published here is wrong, tell us. Corrections are made promptly, the article’s “last updated” date changes, and material corrections are noted at the foot of the piece so the change is visible rather than silent.

Email media@ekoinfrastructure.com with the page, the statement and, where possible, the source you believe is authoritative.

Corrections that touch the regulatory status of EKORails LTD are additionally reviewed by the named compliance officer before the change is published.
